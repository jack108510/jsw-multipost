FAQ

Questions?

Is this safe for my Facebook account? +
The extension simulates human posting — typing into the composer and clicking Post. Use reasonable delays (30s+) between groups to avoid rate limiting. We recommend starting with 5-10 groups at a time.
Does it work on mobile? +
It works on desktop Chrome and Edge. Mobile browsers don't support extensions with content scripts.
Where is my API key stored? +
Locally in Chrome's extension storage. It never touches any server except the AI provider you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) — directly from your browser.
Can I schedule posts to run automatically? +
Yes. Set up daily, hourly, weekly, or one-time schedules in the Schedule tab. Your browser needs to be open for the alarm to fire.
Will Facebook ban me? +
Use sensible delays, don't post to 50 groups in 30 seconds, and you'll be fine. The AI variation feature helps too — unique posts per group look natural.
